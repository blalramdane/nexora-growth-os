# ICP — Restaurants & Cafes

## Primary segment
Egyptian restaurants and cafes that can benefit from digital menu / QR ordering, POS, operational visibility, and WhatsApp-connected workflows.

## Qualification signals
- Busy service periods
- Multiple branches or growth plans
- Manual order/menu workflows
- Active Facebook or Instagram presence
- Delivery/takeaway activity
- Customer communication handled manually

## Required verification
Before outreach, verify the business, official account, relevant pain point, and contact channel.

Do not fabricate missing lead data.
