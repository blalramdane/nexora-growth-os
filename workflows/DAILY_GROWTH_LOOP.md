# NEXORA Daily Growth Loop

## Objective
Create a repeatable daily operating rhythm that turns research into qualified conversations and revenue.

## Daily Loop

### 1. Research
Add new prospects that match the approved ICP.

### 2. Qualify
Verify evidence and calculate lead score.

### 3. Prioritize
Work P1 leads first, then P2.

### 4. Personalize
Draft short outreach tied to a verified business signal.

### 5. Approval
Owner approves outreach before sending.

### 6. Execute
Send approved messages and record activity.

### 7. Follow Up
Review due follow-ups and update pipeline stages.

### 8. Content
Create/publish approved Facebook and Instagram content according to CONTENT_ENGINE.md.

### 9. Measure
Update:
- qualified leads
- conversations
- replies
- demos
- proposals
- wins
- content-assisted leads

### 10. Learn
Record objections, winning messages, lost reasons, and recurring pain points.

## Weekly Optimization
Every week identify:
- best-performing segment
- strongest pain signal
- strongest offer
- highest-converting source
- biggest funnel bottleneck
- one experiment for the next week

## Operating Principle
Research → Evidence → Action → Measurement → Learning.
