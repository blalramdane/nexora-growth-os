# NEXORA Growth KPI Scorecard

## Acquisition
Qualified leads, conversations, response rate, paid CPL when applicable.

## Sales
Demos, proposals, win rate, average deal value, sales cycle.

## Retention
Active customers, churn, expansion, product adoption.

## Content
Reach, engagement, profile visits, inbound conversations, content-assisted leads.

## Weekly review
What generated qualified opportunities? What failed? Which segment/message converted best? What should stop? What should double down?
