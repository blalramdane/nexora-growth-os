# Outreach Playbook

Research → Qualify → Personalize → First message → Follow-up → Qualification → Demo → Proposal → Close → Onboarding

## Rules
- Personalize around a verified business detail.
- Lead with a business problem, not a feature dump.
- Keep first contact short.
- Use one clear CTA.
- Never claim unverified results.
- Drafting is allowed; sending requires owner approval.

## Tracking
Business, contact channel, source, qualification, last contact, next action, owner, outcome.
