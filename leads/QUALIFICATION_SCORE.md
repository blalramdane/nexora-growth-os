# NEXORA Lead Qualification & Scoring

## Goal
Prioritize prospects by probability of becoming a qualified NEXORA opportunity, not by raw lead volume.

## Score: 0–100

### ICP Fit — 0–25
- 25: strong restaurant/cafe fit and clear NEXORA use case
- 15: relevant F&B business but weaker fit
- 5: adjacent business
- 0: outside target

### Pain Signal — 0–20
- 20: verified operational/digital pain
- 10: plausible pain with supporting evidence
- 0: no evidence

### Buying Signal — 0–20
- 20: active expansion, new branch, digital initiative, hiring, or explicit request
- 10: indirect commercial signal
- 0: no signal

### Digital Maturity — 0–15
- 15: active digital presence and customer communication
- 8: basic presence
- 0: weak/inactive presence

### Decision Maker — 0–10
- 10: verified owner/manager/contact
- 5: business contact only
- 0: no usable contact

### Urgency — 0–10
- 10: time-sensitive need
- 5: likely near-term need
- 0: no urgency signal

## Priority
- P1: 80–100 — work first
- P2: 60–79 — active queue
- P3: below 60 — nurture/research

## Rule
A high score does not authorize outreach. Outreach still requires owner approval.
