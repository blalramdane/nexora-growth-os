# NEXORA Lead Research Workflow

Research → Verify → Score → Queue → Personalize

## 1. Discover
Find businesses matching the approved NEXORA restaurant ICP.

## 2. Verify
Confirm:
- Business is active.
- Business category is relevant.
- Public contact channel exists.
- Relevant digital/operational signal exists.

## 3. Capture Evidence
Record the exact public evidence behind:
- pain signal
- buying signal
- branch/expansion signal
- decision-maker information

Do not convert assumptions into facts.

## 4. Score
Apply QUALIFICATION_SCORE.md.

## 5. Queue
Assign P1/P2/P3 and pipeline stage.

## 6. Personalize
Create an outreach draft based only on verified evidence.

## 7. Approval Gate
No message is sent automatically. Owner approval is required before external contact.

## Research Quality Gate
A lead is not considered research-ready unless:
- identity is verified
- source is recorded
- qualification evidence is recorded
- score is explainable
- next action is defined
