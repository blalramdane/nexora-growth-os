# Sales Pipeline

Lead → Qualified → Contacted → Replied → Discovery → Demo → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost

## Required fields
Company, contact, segment, source, stage, pain point, product interest, estimated value, last activity, next action, loss reason.

## Conversion metrics
Lead→Qualified, Qualified→Replied, Replied→Demo, Demo→Proposal, Proposal→Won.

Optimize for qualified opportunities and revenue, not lead volume alone.
