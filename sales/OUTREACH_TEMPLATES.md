# NEXORA Outreach Templates

These are controlled templates, not automatic sending instructions.

## Template A — First Contact
Use when a verified business signal gives a clear reason to contact.

Structure:
1. Personal opening based on verified detail.
2. One relevant business problem.
3. One concise NEXORA outcome.
4. One CTA.

## Template B — Follow-up
Structure:
1. Brief reference to previous contact.
2. Add one useful point or demo angle.
3. One simple CTA.

## Template C — Qualification
Ask only what is needed to understand:
- current workflow
- main pain
- current tools
- decision process
- timing

## Personalization Rules
- Use verified facts only.
- Do not fake familiarity.
- Do not over-explain the product.
- Keep first contact short.
- One CTA per message.
- Do not claim ROI without evidence.

## Approval
All external outreach requires owner approval before sending.
